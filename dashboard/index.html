How the model works

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier × breadth multiplier. The category multiple is the median market-cap-per-TVL of the live-token protocols in that category (at least three of them, else the global median 0.169), clamped to 0.05–1.50. Growth is the 7-day TVL change clamped to −80%…+200% with weight 0.65. Maturity rises with the protocol's age, 0.60 at listing to 1.00 after three years. Breadth adds 2% per chain, up to five chains. No protocol name is special-cased.
